# Roadmap

## Someday

### Native iPhone app (SwiftUI)

Decided 2026-10-24: worth doing eventually, not now.

What it would add over the home-screen web app:

- Buttons inside reminder notifications (done, remind me in an hour), and
  reminders scheduled on the phone so they arrive on time without a server.
- Home-screen and lock-screen widgets with today's tasks.
- Adding tasks through Siri, Shortcuts or the share sheet.
- Native scrolling, gestures and haptics, without Safari's bar above the keyboard.
- Sign in with Apple or iCloud sync instead of email sign-in links.

What it costs: a full rewrite, an Apple Developer account ($99 a year) to keep
it installed, updates through Xcode or TestFlight instead of `git push`, and no
browser or Android version.
